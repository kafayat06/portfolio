// 01 — about

Making code feel like play, not homework.

I'm a coding tutor with over two years of experience introducing children to programming through Scratch, Python, and robotics. I teach both physically and online, meeting kids and parents wherever they're most comfortable — a classroom, a living room, or a video call.

My focus is kids age 6 and up. At that age, curiosity moves fast and attention spans are short, so every lesson is built around small wins: a sprite that finally moves the way you want, a script that runs without errors, a robot that turns left on command. I've taught through Kidz Tech Academy and Deenway Islamic School, along with smaller group and one-on-one sessions.

  • Patience with beginners — breaking down abstract logic into steps a 6-year-old and a 13-year-old can both follow, at their own pace.
  • Bilingual teaching style — comfortable adapting lessons for both physical classrooms and remote, screen-share sessions.
  • Project-first lessons — kids leave each session with something that works, not just notes.
  • Parent & institution communication — clear progress updates for parents and coordinators alike.

// 02 — what i teach

Three subjects, two ways to learn.

scratch --mode=physical,online

Scratch

Block-based programming for first-time coders. Kids build games, animations, and interactive stories while learning loops, events, and variables without ever seeing a line of text-based code.

Physical & Online

python --mode=physical,online

Python

For kids ready to move past drag-and-drop blocks. We cover real syntax, basic logic, and small projects — calculators, quizzes, and simple games — as a bridge into text-based programming.

Physical & Online

robotics --mode=physical

Robotics

Hands-on building and programming of simple robots. This one only works in person — there's no substitute for physically wiring, assembling, and debugging a robot together.

Physical only

Age focus: 6 years and up, from first-time learners to confident young builders.
